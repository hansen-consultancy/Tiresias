---
id: T-24
title: Restoring an older vault.json restores an older approved policy
stride: Tampering
likelihood: 2
impact: 3
status: mitigated
issue: 24
control: ASVS V11 Cryptography
---

**Threat.** A program running as the user edits `vault.json` to send a credential to its own
upstream, widen a grant, or point a grant at another secret.

**Today.** Each secret is bound to its policy inside its ciphertext when a change is approved with
Hello, and the broker refuses a secret under any other policy or unbound
([design](../policy-integrity.md)). Within a lease the broker refuses a vault whose secrets are
older than ones it already saw. What remains is rollback while locked or across a restart: an
older `vault.json` holds an older policy that was once approved, and still works from the next
unlock.

**Closes it.** A high-water mark kept where the user's account can't write, which comes with the
Windows service.
